## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains customer transaction data with the following attributes:

* **User_ID** – Unique customer ID
* **Cust_name** – Customer name
* **Product_ID** – Product identifier
* **Gender** – Male/Female
* **Age Group** – Age category (e.g., 18–25, 26–35)
* **Age** – Exact age
* **Marital_Status** – 0 = Unmarried, 1 = Married
* **State** – Customer location
* **Zone** – Region (North, South, East, West)
* **Occupation** – Customer profession
* **Product_Category** – Purchased category
* **Orders** – Number of orders
* **Amount** – Total purchase amount
* **Status** – Order status
* **unnamed1** – Dropped during preprocessing

## 📈 Key Insights

* Female customers contributed more to overall revenue
* Age group **26–35** showed highest purchasing activity
* Western zone generated maximum sales
* Married customers had higher spending behavior
* Top categories: **Auto, Electronics, Clothing**
* Professionals like Healthcare & IT showed frequent purchases

---

## 📌 Conclusion

The Diwali Sales Analysis project successfully explored customer purchase behavior and sales trends during the festive season. The analysis revealed a significant surge in sales driven by festive demand, discounts, and promotional offers. Customers aged 26–35 were the most active buyers, and female customers contributed a major share of the total revenue.

Regional analysis highlighted that the Western zone generated the highest sales, while product category insights showed strong demand for Auto, Electronics, and Clothing items. Additionally, occupation-based analysis indicated that working professionals such as those in Healthcare and IT sectors made frequent purchases. Married customers also demonstrated higher spending patterns.

Overall, this project demonstrates how data analysis can provide valuable business insights. These insights can help organizations optimize inventory, design targeted marketing strategies, and improve customer engagement, ultimately leading to increased revenue during festive seasons.
